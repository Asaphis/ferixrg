# FerixRG Design Directions

## Three considered approaches

### Quiet Instrument Panel
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial control room that pairs intelligent diagnostics with tactile operational clarity. It feels calm, expensive, and unmistakably built for people making consequential storefront decisions.

**Probability:** 0.06

### Electric Observatory
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, high-contrast analytics universe where data, storefront captures, and AI suggestions appear as luminous observations. It conveys velocity and technical power without becoming a cyberpunk cliché.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Retail Atelier
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, craft-led design studio that treats storefront optimization as an act of retail direction. It feels bespoke and visual, with a gallery-like rhythm rather than software density.

## Selected direction: Quiet Instrument Panel

### Design Movement
**Swiss editorial design meets contemporary industrial dashboards.** FerixRG will have the confidence and information discipline of a premium business publication, brought to an operational workspace with tactile panels and restrained technical detail.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before decoration:** Screenshots, measurements, issue context, and clear status always carry the visual focus.
2. **Calm hierarchy:** Large typography, substantial whitespace, and a stable left-edge alignment make dense information readable.
3. **Tactile precision:** Fine grain, hairline rules, high-quality shadows, and purposeful controls make the product feel considered rather than generic.
4. **Progressive concentration:** The interface begins broad and settles into focused inspections, so complexity is revealed in a deliberate sequence.

### Color Philosophy
FerixRG will use a warm mineral foundation—bone, cloud-gray, and graphite—to create trust and reduce dashboard fatigue. A deeply saturated cobalt blue, **Ferix Blue**, is reserved for decisions, active states, and the “intelligence in motion” moments. Coral and olive are semantic signals, not ornamental colors: they explain risk and progress without turning the system into a traffic-light interface.

### Layout Paradigm
The public product story uses an **asymmetric editorial rail**: a narrow navigation and context strip anchors a generous evidence field rather than centering everything in a generic container. The app continues this logic with a thin operational sidebar, a broad work surface, and context panels that slide in from the right. Visual weight deliberately leans left, leaving space for previews, annotations, and consequence.

### Signature Elements
1. **Evidence ribbons:** Slim annotated strips that join an insight to its score, affected device, or corrective action.
2. **Calibration rings:** Fine-lined score circles whose segmented marks suggest scanning and measured progress.
3. **Cobalt field cards:** Select moments of dark-cobalt surface provide high-contrast anchors for a health score, a pending decision, or a primary action.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every action should describe its consequence before it commits. Dense screens use drawers and focused panels rather than page jumps; selections expose the exact evidence they affect. Buttons react with a slight press, selection borders move with purpose, and simulation outcomes appear with clear, truthful labels.

### Animation
Motion should feel measured rather than theatrical. Evidence cards enter with 40–60ms staggered opacity and upward transform transitions; score rings subtly draw when a completed scan first appears. Drawers use a 220ms custom ease-out from their source edge. Hovers use 140–180ms transform and shadow changes, while keyboard interactions stay instant. All nonessential movement is suppressed for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Manrope** serves as the precise utility sans for controls, data, and body copy; **DM Serif Display** adds editorial authority to product statements, report titles, and key score headlines. App headings use Manrope at 600–750 weight with compact tracking. Display type is used sparingly and only where a decision benefits from an editorial pause; it is never used inside dense tables or toolbars.

### Brand Essence
**FerixRG is the evidence-led storefront intelligence studio for e-commerce teams that want to improve with confidence, not guesswork.**

Personality: **exact, composed, illuminating**.

### Brand Voice
The voice is candid and diagnostically useful. Headlines name the decision; CTAs explain the next movement. It avoids generic aspiration, superlatives, and vague AI language.

Example headline: “The mobile issue is visible. The next move is clear.”

Example CTA: “Inspect the evidence”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a bold, monoline **F/R calibration glyph**: a vertical cobalt axis intersected by two offset radiating arcs, representing a storefront rendered, measured, and refined. The wordmark pairs a custom wide-capital “FERIX” with compact “RG” in a cobalt capsule, so it reads as a product instrument rather than a default text treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Ferix Blue — #155EEF.** This decisive cobalt creates a recognizable visual anchor in an otherwise warm, restrained system.

## Style Decisions

- Serif display type appears only at decisive editorial pauses. The primary hierarchy comes from scale, alignment, weight, and measured whitespace.
- Ferix Blue (#155EEF) is reserved for active navigation, primary decisions, score movement, selected evidence, and consequential annotations rather than general decoration.
- Every application inspection surface carries an instrument-specific evidence motif, such as a viewport tag, calibration mark, measured metadata, or attached-evidence ribbon.
